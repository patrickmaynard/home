Patrick Maynard

I'm Patrick Maynard, a developer, freelance writer and language teacher. The creator of three open-source libraries, I've also contributed freelance writing to more than a dozen publications, including VICE, The Independent and Truthout.
Living and working as a developer in Europe since 2017, I've been an English language instructor for EdCentre Vsetín and have also led events in Berlin for learners of the German language.
Before that, I spent nearly a decade working for The Baltimore Sun.

I've written from a dozen countries on three continents, including a trip to Ukraine in 2025 for Isthmus to write about the life of the bomb maker, mathematician and arguable war criminal Stanisław Ulam.
Interests outside of writing and programming include learning languages, hiking and exploring transit systems.
