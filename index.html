Patrick Maynard

I'm Patrick Maynard. I've been a developer, scribbler and language teacher at one time or another. I've contributed freelance writing to more than a dozen publications, including VICE, The Philadelphia Inquirer and The Independent.
I've also created three open-source libraries for developers.

Living and working in the EU since 2016, I did a one-year stint as an English language instructor for EdCentre in Vsetín at the start of my European experience and have also led events in Berlin for learners of the German language.
Before that, I spent nearly a decade working for The Baltimore Sun in Baltimore, Maryland, USA.

I've written from just under a dozen countries on three continents, including a trip to Ukraine in 2025 for Isthmus and Nature to research the life of the bomb maker, math prodigy and arguable war criminal Stanisław Ulam.
Interests outside of writing, teaching and programming include learning languages, hiking and exploring transit systems.
