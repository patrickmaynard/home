Patrick Maynard

Hi. I'm Patrick Maynard. I've enjoyed being a developer, word scribbler and language teacher at one time or another for over two decades. My freelance writing has appeared in more than a dozen publications, including VICE News, The Philadelphia Inquirer and The Independent.

In the area of programming, I've created and help maintain three open-source code libraries.

At the beginning of my now-decade-long EU experience, I did a one-year stint as an English instructor for EdCentre, a private language school in Vsetín, a small Czech city. I have also led events in Berlin for people learning German.
Before coming to Europe, I spent a decade working for The Baltimore Sun and other newspapers in the United States.

I now live in Prešov, Slovakia.

I've written from just under a dozen countries on three continents, including time spent in Ukraine in 2025 for Isthmus researching the life of the bombmaker, math prodigy and arguable war criminal Stanisław Ulam.

Interests outside of writing, teaching and programming include learning languages, hiking and exploring transit systems.

Originally from Michigan, I'm almost always up for a lake swim.
